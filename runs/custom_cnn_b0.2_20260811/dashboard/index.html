How the ranking works

The table sorts by your first priority. Two rows count as tied when their numbers are very close, because the Pi gives slightly different results each time you run it. Tied rows get a ≈, and your second priority decides which one goes first. There is no single overall score, because working one out would mean deciding how much each thing matters, and that is your call to make.
