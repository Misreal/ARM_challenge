How the ranking works

The table sorts by whatever you put first. If two rows are close enough that the Pi's normal run-to-run wobble could explain the gap, they're marked ≈ and count as tied — your second priority breaks the tie. There's no single overall score, because how much each thing matters is your call, not ours.
